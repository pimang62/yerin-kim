RetroPlanner is an LLM-based retrosynthesis planning system that performs multi-route expansion and multi-objective reasoning. Rather than committing to a single pathway, RetroPlanner jointly compares multiple candidate routes to steer the search toward higher synthesizability, as measured by reaction plausibility, round-trip consistency, and starting-material price. This joint, multi-objective view improves both the discovery and the quality of synthetic routes over existing approaches.
